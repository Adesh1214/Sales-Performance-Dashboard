# Sales-Performance-Dashboard
This project focuses on analyzing sales performance across various dimensions, including time, geography, product categories, and customer segmentation. It uses a structured Excel workbook to provide actionable insights and data visualizations, making it a valuable tool for business decision-making.

Sales Data: Contains detailed transactional data, including order details, customer information, regional data, and product categories. This forms the core dataset for analysis.

Sales Trend: Summarizes sales performance over time, useful for identifying patterns or seasonal trends.

Sales by Region: Breaks down sales by geographical regions, highlighting regional performance and areas of strength or improvement.

Product Share: Shows the contribution of different products to overall sales, helping to identify top-performing products.

Sales by Segmentation: Groups sales data based on specific customer or market segments, enabling targeted strategies.

Sales by State: Provides state-level insights, which is crucial for localized decision-making.


# Data Story - This project narrates the journey of a business through its sales data, uncovering insights like:
Seasonal trends driving revenue.
Regions that outperform or underperform.
Key products contributing to profitability.
Segments of customers that generate the highest returns.
